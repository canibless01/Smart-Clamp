## Source reconciliation

1. **Visual design:** later direct owner approval overrides the attachments' demand for distinct bright shapes/colours or a total navigation redesign. Keep the clean light/sage/forest UI, subtle channel tints and clear hierarchy.
2. **Scope:** the supplement says all phases at once; the owner subsequently accepted the focused launch and explicit deferrals listed above. Retain every deferred row in the inventory.
3. **Detection:** zero current with relay ON, noisy readings, no heartbeat or a changed cell tower are symptoms that may have innocent causes. They are not proof of theft or relocation. Drift needs aligned topology/time/coverage, calibration and persistence. Wording remains review/investigation.
4. **Public naming:** no public buzzer or automatic public accusation. Neutral notices only; naming stays disabled pending a reviewed explicit policy/verification/dispute decision.
5. **Privacy:** anonymous meter validation remains masked; do not reveal a full customer's name/address to satisfy a source example. Private reports/financial control belong to the resident/account holder, not automatically to a physical device owner in an occupied room.
6. **Installer bootstrap:** the device needs a secure bootstrap identity before production keys are issued. A first unauthenticated Internet contact is not authority to reveal a key or commission hardware. One-time key delivery is privileged, confidential and never logged.
7. **Installer completion:** a heartbeat alone is not proof of safe/calibrated installation. Safety sign-off and qualified diagnostics remain prerequisites; deferred payout rules cannot override these safety/identity boundaries.
8. **Realtime:** the implemented sanctioned path is authenticated scoped SSE. Do not weaken the verified live RLS policies simply to replace it with direct Supabase subscriptions. A direct Realtime alternative needs its own audited projection/policy and explicit migration/test decision.
9. **No-meter settlement:** support running and per-bill modes, but activation requires approved custody/evidence/loss policy. Utility payment is human-confirmed, not an invented NEPA payment API. Pass-through pricing does not silently add a landlord margin.
10. **Business configuration:** monetary amounts, programme switches, operating thresholds, timeboxes and retention rules need typed/versioned settings or validated environment defaults. Secrets remain in secure deployment storage. Cryptographic validation, protocol representation and rated hardware bounds are enforced constraints, not freely editable switches.
