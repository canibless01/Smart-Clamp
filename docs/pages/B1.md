# B1. Device Detail and Diagnostics
Route `/devices/[id]`. Audience: customer/assigned installer. Foundation addition to close an implicit product flow; not an extra approved hardware product.

## Content in reading order
Identity/profile and installed capabilities; topology summary; current supply/connectivity/feedback separately; calibration status and last seen; outage and firmware history; user-safe diagnostic results.

## Buttons and behavior
Inspect history; share read-only access; report device problem; assigned installer run diagnostic.

GET authorized device/projection; never export device keys or peer secrets. Customer diagnostics are read-only; installer tests require assigned job and controlled commissioning.
