# B7. Bank Transfer and Payment Instructions
Route `/payments/transfer/[quote_id]`. Audience: payer/status-token holder. Foundation addition to close an implicit product flow; not an extra approved hardware product.

## Content in reading order
Provider-generated destination/account/reference; exact quoted amount/expiry; fee and unit breakdown; expected confirmation; pending/unknown resolution.

## Buttons and behavior
Copy account/reference; check payment status; contact support.

Destination comes from signed provider setup, never hardcoded personal account. Expired quote cannot silently reprice received funds. Caller claim “I paid” requests reconciliation, never credits wallet.
